Hi! I'm a second-year master's student doing applied math at CSUN! I'm broadly interested in numerical modeling and simulation (e.g., for computational physics and fluid dynamics), and computational geometry and topology.

Right now, I'm working with Dr. Alexander Alekseenko on speeding up the computation of the Boltzmann collision operator using the Canonical Polyadic Decomposition for tensors.

I also worked with Dr. Csaba Tóth on improving the bound on the number of crossings for geometric (1+ε)-spanners. I presented our results at GD2026; a link to the conference proceedings is on my writing page.

Previously, I was a backend developer and research intern at the Digital Archaeology Lab at UCLA. I wrote a couple of tutorials for software and other procedures I learned on the job. I also have a writeup exploring image segmentation on images of artifacts.

Besides that, I wrote robust and well-documented scripts so people can import lots of messy data into their databases easier, and I even designed a database or two myself! My biggest project was a digital escape room app based on the Odyssey, which I worked on under my mentor Deidre Brin and Classics Professor Dr. Sarah Beckmann. Check out the demo!
